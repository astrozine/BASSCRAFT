Setup & Tutorials.

Mounting the cushion, placing the controller, and wiring it all in — plus what the three knobs actually do. Full video walkthroughs are coming (a QR code in the box links straight to them); until then, here's the complete process in photos and diagrams.

What's real here: the cushion, controller, and port photos below are BASSCRAFT's actual product photography. The one diagram — how the cushion cables route out of sight — is a schematic, since that step hasn't been filmed yet. Everything here gets replaced by the real video guides once they're shot.

01

Secure the cushion to your seat

1
2
3

Three points hold the cushion in place, so it can't shift once you're on the road — no tools needed for any of them.

  1. Headrest hook. Slips directly over your headrest posts. This is what carries the cushion's weight and keeps it from sliding down.
  2. Side buckle strap. Wraps around the seat back and clips in, cinching the cushion flush against the seat so there's no gap or wobble.
  3. Bottom rail clip. Anchors under the seat rail at the base, stopping the bottom of the cushion from kicking forward under hard braking.
02

Place the controller

The controller sits in your center console or cupholder — anywhere within easy reach of the driver's seat. It's small enough to tuck out of the way, but the three knobs stay accessible while you're driving.

Everything connects on its back panel — power, your cushion cable(s), and an optional direct audio feed. All covered in Step 3.

03

Connect the cables

Four ports on the back panel, left to right:

  1. USB-C — power (or use the 12V cigarette adapter instead; see Power below).
  2. Audio line-in — an optional direct feed from your car's audio system, if you'd rather not rely on the onboard microphone.
  3. Cushion cable — seat 1
  4. Cushion cable — seat 2 (Double Kit only)

Port order shown left to right in the photo above; final silkscreen labels on the unit are still pending confirmation.

Routing the cushion cables

Each cushion cable runs from the controller, along the seat frame, and tucks into the fold where the seat back meets the seat base — roughly where your lower back and tailbone rest. Once it's in, it's completely out of sight and out of the way.

●

The three controls

FREQ

Frequency

Dials in the frequency range the cushion's haptics focus on — tune it toward the low end a specific track or genre lives in.

PWR / INTENSITY

Power & Intensity

Turns the system on, then keeps adjusting from there — turn it up for more strength, down for a lighter touch.

NOISE CTRL

Noise Gate

Filters out background road and engine noise picked up by the microphone, so it's your music driving the haptics — not traffic.

●

Power

Plug in wherever's easiest — a USB-C port or a standard 12V cigarette lighter both work. No difference in performance between the two; use whichever your car has free.
