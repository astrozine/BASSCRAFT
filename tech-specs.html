1. The Engine
Dual high-torque haptic transducers deliver precise frequencies (10Hz–200Hz), tucked inside the cushion itself.
Driven by a custom 50W RMS neodymium motor structure, these transducers reach peak excursion in just 4.2ms. The 3.2G of sustained force delivers ultra-responsive kicks, ensuring 98% resonance translation to the human body.
2. Anatomy
Memory foam core, breathable mesh, and embedded transducers — layered for all-day comfort and full-body bass.
Engineered with multi-density foam that absorbs 70% of vehicle road vibrations while channeling 100% of the transducer energy forward. The ballistic mesh cover is rated for 50,000 abrasion cycles, offering maximum durability.
3. Controller
The basscraft controller box and frequency sensor houses the amplifier, DAC, and intensity electronics that drive both cushions.
Featuring a 32-bit internal DSP operating at 48kHz, the controller processes low-end frequencies with less than 0.8ms latency. Its Class-D amplifier module guarantees 90% power efficiency, minimizing heat output in closed consoles.
4. Placement
The controller tucks discreetly out of sight — drivers and passengers can each run their own.
Measuring just 4.2" x 2.8" x 1.1", the unit can be mounted in 95% of standard vehicle center consoles, under seats, or inside glove compartments. High-tensile Velcro strips are included for secure mounting up to 5G of cornering force.
5. Precision
Three analog knobs let you adjust intensity by feel — no app, no screen, no distraction.
Machined from solid aluminum, the knobs provide tactile detents every 5 degrees for blind adjustments while driving. The potentiometers are rated for 100,000 turns and deliver a perfectly linear control curve.
6. Wiring
Familiar, standard cable types — fully wired for zero-latency response. No Bluetooth to pair, drop, or lag.
The 3.5mm TRS connections feature gold-plated contacts preventing oxidation over a 10-year lifespan. The 6-foot heavy-gauge braided cables boast a 50lb tensile strength, ensuring they won't pinch or fray in seat tracks.
7. Power
Plug into any 12V cigarette outlet or a USB-C port — universal power for any vehicle.
The system accepts a wide input range from 10V to 15V. Using the supplied 12V adapter delivers a peak output of 5A, while the USB-C option requires a Power Delivery (PD) rated port of at least 30W for maximum excursion.
8. Zero Lag
Lag is the enemy of rhythm — that's why BASSCRAFT runs purely on a wired connection.
Wireless audio protocols inherently introduce 40-120ms of latency, destroying the psychoacoustic sync between hearing a beat and feeling it. Our fully analog signal path ensures absolute 0ms delay, perfectly locking the haptics to the car's sound system.
