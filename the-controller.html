The Controller
Control intensity, frequency, and noise gating from a compact, intuitive interface.
Featuring a 32-bit internal DSP operating at 48kHz, the controller processes low-end frequencies with less than 0.8ms latency. Its Class-D amplifier module guarantees 90% power efficiency, minimizing heat output in closed consoles.
Tactile Control
Three analog knobs give you immediate, eyes-free control over your experience. Adjust frequency range, noise gating, and overall intensity without ever looking down at a screen.
Machined from solid aluminum, the knobs provide tactile detents every 5 degrees for blind adjustments while driving. The potentiometers are rated for 100,000 turns and deliver a perfectly linear control curve.
Zero-Lag Connectivity
Everything routes cleanly through the rear panel. Power it via the included 12V adapter or any standard USB-C port. Hardwired audio connections guarantee zero latency between the beat and the bass.
The 3.5mm TRS connections feature gold-plated contacts preventing oxidation over a 10-year lifespan. Our fully analog signal path ensures absolute 0ms delay, perfectly locking the haptics to the car's sound system.
Placement Options
Compact enough to tuck into a center console, cupholder, or door pocket. The built-in ambient microphone still picks up the music even when stowed out of sight.
Measuring just 4.2" x 2.8" x 1.1", the unit can be mounted in 95% of standard vehicle center consoles. High-tensile Velcro strips are included for secure mounting up to 5G of cornering force.

Where the Controller Goes

The controller tucks discreetly out of sight — drivers and passengers can each run their own. Pinch, or use the numbers and +/− controls, to zoom into suggested locations for the cushion controller.
