Hear from BASSCRAFT owners

You hear the bass. You never feel it.

Factory speakers move air. They don't move you. And every real fix costs you your trunk, your weekend, or your neighbours.

Factory speakers stop short

Stock door speakers roll off long before the low end arrives. The part of the track you're supposed to feel in your chest simply isn't being played.

A real sub costs you the trunk

A proper box eats your cargo space, then asks for an amp, a wiring run, a shop appointment and a few hundred dollars before it makes a sound.

Loud is not the same as deep

Turning it up just pushes the mids into distortion and broadcasts your playlist to the entire street. Volume was never the thing you were missing.

Headphones aren't an option

The one place you actually want the low end wrapped around you is the one place you can't wear them.

So we stopped trying to make the car louder — and made the seat itself the speaker.
